5.1.2.1. Introduction

5.1.2.2. Reference and Lexical Works
  • The Brown-Driver-Briggs Hebrew and English Lexicon by Francis Brown, R. Driver, and Charles Briggs is widely considered the finest and most comprehensive Hebrew lexicon for English-speaking students.
  • Phonology and Morphology of Biblical Hebrew by Joshua Blau is regarded by many as the ultimate scholarly read for understanding the sound system and word structure of the ancient language.
  • The Analytical Hebrew and Chaldee Lexicon by Benjamin Davidson offers grammatical analysis and word meanings by derivation, aiding beginners in identifying root words.
  • A Practical Grammar for Classical Hebrew by J. Weingreen provides a comprehensive guide to grammar and syntax for both beginners and advanced learners.
  • A Grammar of Biblical Hebrew by Paul Joüon and T. Muraoka is a complete, updated single-volume grammar that accounts for modern developments in Hebrew linguistics.
5.1.2.3. Textbooks for Learning Modern Hebrew
  • The Routledge Introductory Course in Modern Hebrew is frequently cited as the best overall book for learning Modern Hebrew, featuring 90 lessons with grammar, dialogues, and cultural notes.
  • Hebrew From Scratch is a popular series that delves into core grammar elements, verb conjugations, and sentence structure with practical examples.
  • HaYesod by Luba Uveeler is a classic textbook known for its systematic approach to grammar and vocabulary, often used in academic settings like UCLA.
  • Brandeis Modern Hebrew by Vardit Ringvald, Bonit Porath, Yaron Peleg, Esther Shorr, and Sara Hascal is recommended for beginner to intermediate learners.
  • Modern Hebrew Verbs Step By Step helps learners understand verb structure and tenses, making it a top choice for mastering Hebrew grammar logic.
5.1.2.4. Textbooks for Learning Biblical Hebrew
  • The First Hebrew Primer by Ethelyn Simon, Linda Motzkin, and Irene Resnikoff is a standard text for adult beginners, guiding readers from basic pronunciation to reading entire Bible chapters.
  • Basics of Biblical Hebrew by Gary D. Pratico and Miles V. Van Pelt is a widely used textbook and workbook set for self-teaching and classroom use.
  • These Are The Words: Essential Biblical Hebrew Vocabularies is designed to help students quickly learn Bible vocabulary with correct prefixes, pronouns, and tense particles.
  • Learning Biblical Hebrew by Kutz and Josberger is highlighted by some educators as a favorite resource for its clear explanations.
5.1.2.5. Notable Hebrew Literature and Culture
  • Only Yesterday by S.Y. Agnon is a masterpiece of modern Hebrew literature by a Nobel Laureate, detailing the immigration to Palestine in the early 20th century.
  • A Tale of Love and Darkness by Amos Oz is a celebrated autobiographical novel translated into English.
  • A Horse Walks Into a Bar by David Grossman is a prominent work of contemporary Israeli fiction.
  • The Kite Runner by Khaled Hosseini is listed in some rankings, though it is originally in English and set in Afghanistan; its inclusion often reflects its popularity among readers of Middle Eastern themes rather than its Hebrew origin.
  • Memories After My Death by Yair Lapid is a memoir about his father, Tommy Lapid, offering insights into modern Israeli political history.
